# 📄 Product One-Pager: Credalytix

## **Working Capital Eligibility Calculator & Institutional Credit Appraisal Suite**

**Current Product Stage:** `Active Testing & Pilot Phase (Closed Beta)`

---

### 📌 Problem Statement
Commercial loan underwriting for SMEs and mid-market enterprises takes **14 to 21 days**. Credit underwriters spend hours manually transcribing multi-year financial statements into disparate spreadsheets. Subjective risk assessment models and fragmented compliance checks result in delayed turnarounds, underwriter fatigue, and elevated Non-Performing Assets (NPAs).

---

### 💡 The Solution
**Credalytix** is an institutional-grade B2B credit appraisal platform designed for commercial banks, NBFCs, private credit funds, and FinTech lenders. It completely automates financial statement spreading, ratio diagnostics, Risk Assessment Model (RAM) scoring, regulatory working capital (MPBF) appraisal, and Credit Appraisal Memo (CAM) generation in **under 15 minutes**.

---

### 🌟 Core Capabilities & Value Proposition

| Capability | What It Does | Business Impact |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Proprietary RAM Scorecard** | Calibrated scoring model across Financial Performance, Capital Structure, and Business Fundamentals. | Objective, standardized, and auditable credit risk rating (Tier 1 to Tier 5). |
| **Multi-Year Financial Diagnostics** | 3-year comparative balance sheet & P&L spreading with real-time accounting balance validation. | 100% elimination of calculation errors and instant ratio trend detection. |
| **Schedule Completeness & Fallback Engine** | Granular extraction of CWIP, Intangibles, Trade Payables, and schedule tracking (Debtor Aging, Inventory, Fixed Assets). | Prevents deal roadblocks; provides smart fallback scores with contextual advice. |
| **Regulatory MPBF Working Capital** | Implements Tandon Committee Method II, Net Working Capital (NWC) gaps, and Drawing Power computations. | Eliminates regulatory non-compliance in working capital sanction limits. |
| **Collateral & SARFAESI Assessment** | Automated asset-specific LTV haircuts, property valuation cover, and security shortfall calculations. | Ensures strict asset-backed credit protection. |
| **One-Click CAM Export** | Generates institutional-grade Credit Appraisal Memos with underwriting verdicts and conditionality lists. | Compresses loan approval cycle by **95%** (14 days ➔ 15 mins). |

---

### 🎯 Target Customers (ICPs)
1. **Public & Private Sector Commercial Banks:** Standardize branch underwriting and eliminate spreadsheet fragmentation.
2. **NBFCs & MSME Lenders:** Accelerated credit decisions to capture prime borrowers before competitors.
3. **FinTech Lenders & Neobanks:** Embed institutional credit underwriting APIs directly into digital lending flows.
4. **Loan Syndication Desks & DSAs:** Generate pre-qualified bank-ready credit dossiers for SME clients.

---

### 📈 Business Model
- **Enterprise SaaS:** Annual tiered licensing per credit underwriter seat ($50k – $250k/year).
- **Transaction / API Pricing:** $10 – $35 per completed loan dossier for digital lenders.
- **On-Premise Private Cloud:** Dedicated deployments for regulated scheduled commercial banks.
